999920 Meaning: 久久久久爱你

Quick answer: 999920 means “love you forever and ever.” Each 9 (九, jiǔ) sounds like 久 (jiǔ, “long-lasting”), and 20 (èr líng) sounds like 爱你 (ài nǐ, “love you”).

Read aloud, jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ èr líng echoes jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ ài nǐ — 久久久久爱你. Repetition is an intensifier in Chinese: 久久 already means “for a long, long time”; doubling it again turns it into “forever and ever”.

Why 9 means forever

Nine (九) is a near-perfect homophone of 久, “long-lasting”. As the largest single digit, it also came to symbolise completeness and the emperor. Imperial robes carried nine dragons, palace gates were studded with 81 (9×9) golden nails, and legend says the Forbidden City was built with 9,999½ rooms — one half-room short of heaven's 10,000.

The ninth day of the ninth lunar month is the Double Ninth Festival (重阳节), a day honouring longevity. And in modern romance, 9 is the go-to number for commitment: 99 or 999 roses, 9,999-yuan red envelopes, and weddings booked on 9/9.

Why 20 means “love you”

2 (èr) is read as 爱 (ài, love) and 0 (líng) as 你 (nǐ, you). It's the same “20” that ends the world-famous 520 — 我爱你, “I love you”. You'll also see it in 1920 (依旧爱你, still love you) and 9420 (就是爱你, it's you I love).

9999 in weddings, gold and palaces

  • Gold purity: “9999” (999.9, 千足金) marks the highest common fineness of 24K gold — the standard for Chinese wedding bangles, dragon-and-phoenix sets and investment bars. See our gold guide.
  • Red envelopes: grooms and families traditionally give 9,999 or 99,999 yuan to wish a long-lasting marriage.
  • Flowers: 99 roses = “forever”, 999 roses = “eternal love”.
  • Architecture: the “9,999½ rooms” legend of the Forbidden City (the actual count is lower, but the symbolism stuck).

The economy of love numbers

Number codes aren't just slang — they steer calendars and wallets. China registered 6.763 million marriages in 2025, up 10.76% year-on-year, and on May 20 (520) registration slots in cities like Hangzhou and Guangzhou book out completely. On the 520 shopping peak, Douyin reported “520” searches and purchases up 1.4×, with perfume sales +186% and lipstick +143% year-on-year. Chinese consumers spent RMB 84 billion on gold jewellery in Q1 2025, and weddings remain a key gold-buying moment.

For couples and brands worldwide, that means love codes are a calendar: 520, 521, 9/9, Qixi and Valentine's are high-intent moments for proposals, weddings, jewellery and gifts.

How to use 999920

  1. Text it: send “999920” as a secret “love you forever”. Make a card.
  2. Engrave it: inside a ring, bracelet or watch — six digits, one vow.
  3. Gift it: 9 or 99 roses with a note reading 999920; a 999.9 gold keepsake.
  4. Time it: propose or marry on a date rich in 9s. Find one.
  5. Gift money: 999.20 or 9,999.20 in a red envelope — forever, plus love.

FAQ

What is 999920 in Chinese characters?

久久久久爱你 (jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ ài nǐ), meaning “love you forever and ever.”

Can I say 999920 to a friend?

It's strongly romantic. For friends, use 918 (加油吧, “you've got this”) or 666 (awesome) instead.

Is “9999” gold the same as 24K?

999.9 (“four nines”) is the purest common grade of 24K gold, at 99.99% fine gold content. Some 24K items are 999 (99.9%).

Does 999920 have any unlucky digits?

No. It contains no 4, the digit avoided because it sounds like “death.”
