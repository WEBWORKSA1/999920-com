The meaning

Why 999920 is the ultimate love code

Chinese is rich in homophones, so numbers double as words. The number 9 is the highest single digit and was the emperor's number — the Forbidden City is said to hold “9,999½ rooms”. Grooms traditionally give red envelopes of 9,999 and bouquets of 99 or 999 roses to wish for a love that lasts.

Stack four nines — 久久久久, “forever and ever” — and finish with 20, 爱你, “love you”. The result is a vow in six digits.

Frequently asked questions

What does 999920 mean in Chinese?

999920 is read as 久久久久爱你 (jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ jiǔ ài nǐ): 9 sounds like 久 “long-lasting/forever” and 20 sounds like 爱你 “love you” — so it means “love you forever and ever.”

Is 999920 a lucky number?

Yes. It is built from the number 9, a symbol of longevity and completeness, and the love code 20. It carries no 4s, the digit usually avoided because it sounds like “death.”

How is 999920 different from 5201314?

5201314 says “I love you for a lifetime” (我爱你一生一世). 999920 flips the emphasis: it leads with forever (久久久久) and ends with “love you.” Both are popular for messages, engraving and dates.
