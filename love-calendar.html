FAQ

When is 520 Day?

Every year on May 20, because 5-20 sounds like 我爱你, “I love you.” May 21 (521, 我愿意, “I'm willing”) is its reply.

When is Qixi 2027?

Qixi, the Chinese Valentine's Day on the 7th day of the 7th lunar month, falls on August 8, 2027.
