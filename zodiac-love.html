FAQ

Which zodiac signs are the best match?

Traditionally the Six Harmonies pairs (such as Rat and Ox, or Dragon and Rooster) are the most harmonious, followed by animals in the same Trine.

Can clashing signs have a happy marriage?

Of course. Zodiac matching is a cultural tradition, not destiny; many families simply choose an auspicious wedding date to balance a clash.
