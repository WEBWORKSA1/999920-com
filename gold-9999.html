9999 Gold: the “Forever” Metal 🪙

“9999” stamped on gold means 999.9 fineness — 99.99% pure. In Chinese it's 千足金, and it's the gold of weddings, dowries and heirlooms.

Gold in Chinese weddings

  • Dragon-and-phoenix bangles (龙凤镯) — symbols of a balanced, prosperous marriage, often given by the groom's family.
  • Betrothal gold sets — traditionally a set of necklace, earrings, ring and bangle (“three gold” or “five gold” sets).
  • Tea ceremony gifts — elders gift gold jewellery to the couple during the tea ceremony.
  • Red envelopes — cash gifts in lucky amounts such as 999, 9,999 or 8,888.
Gold remains a top pick for self-reward and gifting: 81% of surveyed Chinese consumers owned fine gold jewellery in 2025, up from 62% in 2019 (World Gold Council).

FAQ

What does 9999 mean on gold?

It means 999.9 fineness: 99.99% pure gold, the highest common grade of 24K gold.

Is 9999 gold good for rings?

It is very soft, so it scratches and bends easily. For daily-wear rings, 18K or 22K is more durable; 9999 is ideal for bangles, pendants and keepsakes.

How many grams in a tael?

A Hong Kong gold tael is 37.429 grams; the mainland Chinese market tael (市两) is 50 grams.
