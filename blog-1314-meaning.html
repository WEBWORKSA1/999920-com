1314 & 5201314: The Lifetime Codes

Quick answer: 1314 (yī sān yī sì) sounds like 一生一世 (yī shēng yī shì) — “one life, one lifetime.” 5201314 = 我爱你一生一世 — “I love you for a lifetime.”

Breaking down 1314

1一one
3生life
1一one
4世lifetime

Here 4 is read as 世 (shì, lifetime) rather than the unlucky 死 — context changes everything.

Where you'll see 1314

  • Red envelopes: 1,314 or 13,140 yuan for weddings and Valentine's.

FAQ

Is 1314 unlucky because it has a 4?

No. In 1314 the 4 is read as 世 (lifetime), so the code is considered very romantic.

What is the difference between 1314 and 999920?

1314 means “a lifetime”; 999920 means “love you forever and ever” — stronger on eternity and ending with “love you.”
