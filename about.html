About 999920

Six digits. One vow. 久久久久爱你 — love you forever and ever.

999920.com is an independent website that celebrates the beautiful way Chinese language turns numbers into feelings. We explain love and lucky number codes, build free tools that help people express love, and connect couples and gift-givers with trusted providers for the big moments.

Our mission

  • Explain Chinese number culture accurately and respectfully, in English and Chinese.
  • Empower people to say “I love you” in new ways — with codes, cards, dates and gifts.
  • Connect couples with great jewelers, planners and creators through free, no-obligation quotes.
  • Celebrate real love stories through contests and community.

How we're funded

The site is free. It is supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly-labelled sponsorships, affiliate links, referral fees from providers, and community donations. Sponsors never influence our cultural explanations.

Editorial standards

We cite sources for statistics, label entertainment tools clearly, and correct errors promptly. Spotted a mistake? Tell us.
