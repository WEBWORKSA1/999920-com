What each number means in Chinese culture

1 一

Unity and new beginnings; in codes, 1 often reads as 一 (one) or 依 (rely on).

2 二 → 爱

“Good things come in pairs” (好事成双). In love codes, 2 sounds like 爱, love.

3 三 → 生

Sounds like 生 (life/birth). Found in 3344 (生生世世, life after life).

4 四 → 死

Avoided because 四 (sì) is close to 死 (sǐ, death). Many buildings skip 4th floors. In 1314 it softens to 世, lifetime.

5 五 → 我

The five elements; in codes 5 is 我, “I/me” — as in 520.

6 六 → 顺

Smooth, going well: 六六大顺 “everything goes smoothly”. 666 means “awesome” online.

7 七 → 亲 / 妻

Togetherness; Qixi falls on the 7th day of the 7th month. In codes, 7 is a kiss (亲).

8 八 → 发

The wealth number: 八 (bā) rhymes with 发 (fā, prosper). The 2008 Olympics opened at 8:08:08 pm on 8/8/08.

9 九 → 久

Eternity and the emperor. 9 = 久, forever — the heart of 999920.

0 零 → 你

Wholeness and a fresh start; in codes 0 is 你, “you”.

FAQ

What is the luckiest number in Chinese culture?

8 is the luckiest for wealth because 八 sounds like 发 (prosper). For love and longevity, 9 (久, forever) is the favourite.

Why is 4 unlucky in China?

四 (sì) sounds like 死 (sǐ), death, so 4 is often avoided in phone numbers, floors and gift amounts.

How is my zodiac animal calculated?

By your birth year in the Chinese lunar calendar. If you were born in January or early February, check the date of Lunar New Year that year.
